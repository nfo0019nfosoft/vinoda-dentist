General Dentistry

Comprehensive Oral Exams • Professional Cleaning • Fillings • Preventive Care

About General Dentistry

Our General Dentistry department provides the comprehensive diagnostic and restorative dental care for patients of all ages. From routine oral check-ups and professional cleaning to advanced cavity fillings and gum health management, we ensure painless, ethical, and modern treatment for every smile.

Our General Dentistry Sub-Services

Dental Cleanings

Professional cleaning removes plaque and tartar buildup while protecting your gums. It helps prevent cavities and bad breath, keeping your smile fresh. Regular cleanings are essential for maintaining long-term oral health.

  • Plaque Removal
  • Stain Cleaning
  • Routine Checkup

Dental Sealants

Dental sealants act as a protective layer over teeth to prevent decay and damage. They are especially effective for children and cavity-prone teeth. This quick and painless procedure offers long-lasting protection.

  • Cavity Protection
  • Quick Procedure
  • Long-Lasting Care

Dentistry For Kids

We provide gentle and friendly dental care specially designed for children’s comfort. Our focus is on prevention, education, and building healthy habits early. This helps ensure strong and healthy smiles as they grow.

  • Child-Friendly Care
  • Preventive Treatments
  • Growth Monitoring

Fillings

Dental fillings restore damaged or decayed teeth using strong and natural-looking materials. They help prevent further decay and maintain tooth structure. This ensures durability and proper function for everyday use.

  • Cavity Repair
  • Tooth Protection
  • Durable Results

Laser Assisted Periodontal Therapy

This advanced laser treatment effectively treats gum disease with precision and minimal discomfort. It reduces bleeding and speeds up healing time. Patients benefit from a safer and more comfortable experience.

  • Gum Treatment
  • Less Discomfort
  • Quick Recovery

Oral Cancer Screenings

Oral cancer screenings help detect abnormal tissues early before they become serious. The process is quick, painless, and highly important. Regular screenings improve chances of early treatment and prevention.

  • Early Detection
  • Routine Screening
  • Preventive Care

Root Canal Therapy

Root canal therapy removes infection from inside the tooth and saves your natural structure. It eliminates pain and prevents further damage effectively. This treatment restores normal function and long-term stability.

  • Pain Relief
  • Tooth Saving
  • Long-Term Fix

Scaling & Root Planing

Scaling and root planing is a deep cleaning procedure that removes plaque below the gums. It treats early gum disease and prevents further complications. This improves gum health and supports strong teeth.

  • Deep Cleaning
  • Gum Care
  • Bacteria Removal

Sleep Apnea Treatment

Sleep apnea treatment improves breathing patterns during sleep and reduces snoring issues. It enhances sleep quality and overall health significantly. Custom solutions are designed for maximum comfort and results.

  • Custom Devices
  • Better Sleep
  • Snoring Control

Sleep Dentistry (Nitrous and Oral)

Sleep dentistry uses safe sedation methods to make treatments comfortable and stress-free. It is ideal for anxious or sensitive patients. This approach ensures a relaxed and smooth dental experience.

  • Nitrous Sedation
  • Oral Sedation
  • Comfortable Care

Tooth Extractions

Tooth extraction is performed safely to remove severely damaged or infected teeth. It helps prevent the spread of infection and pain. Modern techniques ensure quick recovery and minimal discomfort.

  • Simple Removal
  • Pain Control
  • Fast Healing

TMJ Therapy

TMJ therapy treats jaw pain, stiffness, and alignment issues effectively. It improves jaw movement and reduces discomfort during daily activities. Customized treatments ensure better long-term relief.

  • Pain Relief
  • Bite Alignment
  • Custom Therapy

Wisdom Teeth Removal

Wisdom teeth removal is done to prevent pain, crowding, and infections in the mouth. Impacted teeth are treated carefully with modern techniques. Recovery is smooth with proper guidance and care.

  • Impacted Removal
  • Pain Prevention
  • Aftercare Support

Cosmetic Dentistry

Teeth Whitening • Veneers • Smile Makeovers • Tooth Reshaping

About Cosmetic Dentistry

Our Cosmetic Dentistry department is dedicated to enhancing the beauty and confidence of your smile. We offer a full range of aesthetic dental treatments, including teeth whitening, veneers, smile makeovers, and tooth reshaping, all tailored to your individual needs. Using advanced techniques and modern technology, we ensure safe, painless, and natural-looking results.

Our Cosmetic Dentistry Sub-Services

Veneers

Enhance your smile with custom-made veneers that cover imperfections like stains, chips, or gaps. They provide a natural and long-lasting aesthetic improvement for a confident look.

  • Smile Makeover
  • Stain & Gap Coverage
  • Natural Appearance

Componeers

Componeers are a minimally invasive cosmetic solution that instantly improves the appearance of your teeth. They are applied directly for quick and effective smile enhancement.

  • Instant Results
  • Minimally Invasive
  • Cost-Effective Option

Dental Crowns and Bridges

Restore damaged or missing teeth with durable crowns and bridges designed for strength and aesthetics. They help maintain proper bite and improve overall oral function.

  • Tooth Restoration
  • Missing Teeth Solution
  • Strong & Durable

Implant Dentures

Secure and comfortable dentures supported by dental implants for improved stability. They offer better chewing ability and a more natural feel compared to traditional dentures.

  • Implant Support
  • Better Stability
  • Enhanced Comfort

E-Max Crowns

E-Max crowns are high-quality ceramic restorations known for their strength and natural look. They are ideal for restoring front teeth with superior aesthetics.

  • Premium Aesthetics
  • High Strength
  • Long-Lasting Results

Orthodontics

Braces • Clear Aligners • Bite Correction • Smile Alignment

About Orthodontics

Our Orthodontics department focuses on correcting misaligned teeth and jaw irregularities to improve both function and appearance. We provide advanced treatments including braces, and preventive orthodontic care for children and adults. With modern techniques and personalized treatment plans, we ensure comfortable, effective, and long-lasting results that enhance your smile and overall oral health.

Our Orthodontic Sub-Services

Clear Correct

ClearCorrect aligners offer a discreet way to straighten teeth without metal braces. They are custom-made for comfort and gradually shift teeth into proper alignment.

  • Invisible Aligners
  • Comfortable Fit
  • Removable Design

Expander Therapy

Expander therapy helps widen the upper jaw to correct bite issues and create space for proper tooth alignment. It is commonly used during early orthodontic treatment.

  • Jaw Expansion
  • Bite Correction
  • Early Treatment

Clear Aligner Therapy

Clear aligners provide a modern solution for straightening teeth without visible braces. They are comfortable, removable, and designed for effective results.

  • Nearly Invisible
  • Easy Maintenance
  • Effective Alignment

Night and Athletic Guards

Custom-made guards protect your teeth from grinding or sports injuries. They provide comfort, durability, and essential protection during sleep or physical activities.

  • Teeth Protection
  • Custom Fit
  • Durable Material

Space Maintainers

Space maintainers help keep space open for permanent teeth when baby teeth are lost early. They guide proper tooth eruption and prevent alignment issues.

  • Prevent Crowding
  • Maintain Space
  • Support Growth

Single Tooth Implants • Full Mouth Implants • Permanent Tooth Replacement • Bone Grafting

About Dental Implants

Dental implants are a permanent and reliable solution for replacing missing teeth. They are designed to look and function like natural teeth while restoring your smile and confidence. Dental Implants

  • Permanent Tooth Replacement
  • Natural Look & Feel
  • Improved Chewing Ability
  • Long-Lasting Solution
